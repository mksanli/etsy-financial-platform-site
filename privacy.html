Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 16, 2026

This page describes the intended data practices for the Seller Financial Intelligence project, a web-based financial analytics and decision-support application for Etsy sellers. The application is currently under development; this website is informational and does not currently provide Etsy account connection or customer registration.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact koksalsanli@gmail.com.

Information collected by this website

This static informational website does not contain a registration form or request Etsy account credentials. If you contact us by email, we receive the information you choose to send. GitHub Pages, which hosts this site, may process technical request information according to its own policies.

Planned application data processing

If the application launches and you choose to connect an Etsy shop, it is intended to request your authorization through Etsy OAuth 2.0 and retrieve available read-only shop information, such as orders, sales transactions, payments, fees, listings, and product information, to provide financial analytics. You may also provide cost data, such as product and shipping expenses. The application will not request your Etsy password.

Purpose and sharing

The intended purpose of processing is to calculate and present shop financial performance and pricing insights. We do not intend to sell identifiable seller or customer information. Authorized service providers may be necessary to operate the future application; details will be provided before the service becomes available.

Retention, security, and your choices

Before Etsy account connections are enabled, this policy will be updated with the implemented data storage locations, retention periods, security measures, service providers, and instructions for revoking access, requesting deletion, and exercising applicable privacy rights. You can contact us now using the email above.

Changes

We may update this policy as development progresses. The latest version and its update date will be published here.

This project is independent of Etsy, Inc. and is not endorsed by Etsy.
